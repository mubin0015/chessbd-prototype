01 / WHO WE ARE

Science, engineering and partnership for a resilient Bangladesh.

The Centre for Humanitarian and Earth Science Studies (CHESS) is a multidisciplinary research and consultancy institution based in Dhaka.

We help governments, development partners, investors and organisations plan, build and manage water, energy, environment and infrastructure programmes that are climate-resilient, compliant and good for communities.

  • Evidence-led thinking
  • Multidisciplinary collaboration
  • Practical, context-aware solutions
